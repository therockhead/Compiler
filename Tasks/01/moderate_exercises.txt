MODERATE LEVEL — Variable Assignments & Expressions
=====================================================
Operators used: =, +=, -=, *=, /=
Each exercise declares variables, combines them with expressions,
and ends with a print() statement. These mirror the same
calculations as the Easy-level exercises, one level higher.

--------------------------------------------------
Exercise 1 — Basic arithmetic
--------------------------------------------------
a = 8
b = a + 12
b *= -5
print(b)

--------------------------------------------------
Exercise 2 — GC content (percentage)
--------------------------------------------------
gc_count = 45
total_len = 100
gc_percent = gc_count / total_len
gc_percent *= 100
print(gc_percent)

--------------------------------------------------
Exercise 3 — Trimmed sequence length
--------------------------------------------------
raw_len = 150
adapter_len = 12
trimmed_len = raw_len - adapter_len
print(trimmed_len)

--------------------------------------------------
Exercise 4 — Sequencing coverage
--------------------------------------------------
reads = 500
read_len = 100
genome_len = 1000
coverage = reads * read_len
coverage /= genome_len
print(coverage)

--------------------------------------------------
Exercise 5 — Protein length from coding sequence
--------------------------------------------------
seq_len = 300
codons = seq_len / 3
protein_len = codons
protein_len -= 1
print(protein_len)
